        Model assumptions & prototype limits

        Illustrative equipment

        One 7.5 kW compressor; 0.50 m³ receiver; nominal air delivery 0.90 m³/min at reference conditions. Pressure controller loads at 6.4 bar(g) and unloads at 7.2 bar(g).

        Air flows use the same 1.01325 bar reference pressure and assumed constant temperature. The receiver balance updates once per simulated second.

        What the results mean

        A production cycle takes 15 seconds. It counts as accepted only when the demo minimum pressure is met throughout the cycle. This is a pressure-only proxy; real product quality is not modelled.

        Monthly figures on the plant overview come from the 14-day calendar (two shifts, Saturday half day, Sunday off), not from repeating one 30-minute run around the clock. The live-twin optimiser cards still extrapolate their 30-minute run 24×7 and say so.

        What is not modelled

        Startup transients, motor restart limits, temperature effects on leak orifices, valve seat leakage during isolation tests and real product quality. Heat-recovery and VSD prices are editable defaults, not quotes.

        The identification needs the nameplate delivery (FAD); a machine delivering below nameplate biases leak and volume estimates by the same few percent, which the validation table shows openly.

        User roles & access

        Role chosen in the sidebar: Maintenance engineer. Operators raise & acknowledge tickets; engineers repair, verify & resolve; managers also change calibration, run stress tests and restore backups. Enforced by the API.

        Backup & restore

        Download calibration, tickets and reports as one ZIP; restore it on any machine. Restore accepts only whitelisted JSON files and validates them first.

        Edge connectivity & integration (Modbus / MQTT)

        FactoryAir Twin is edge-first: one power measurement at the compressor panel (PowerTag-class), one header pressure transmitter and controller state are enough. The edge device publishes readings and detection windows locally; only operator-approved recommendations are acted on.
